Honors & Commendation Policy

1. Eligibility Framework

All registered undergraduate IEEE student members of SLIIT in good financial standing (IEEE global member dues current) are eligible for honors consideration. Volunteers must log verified hours through the VMS platform.

2. Volunteer of the Month Selection

Conferred on the 1st of every month for outstanding performance during the prior calendar month. Nominations are submitted by Chapter Leads or OC Chairs, followed by executive committee consensus.

3. Annual AGM Distinctions

Evaluated annually between October and November based on total verified volunteer hours (weight: 25%), organizing committee leadership (35%), community feedback (20%), and adherence to IEEE Code of Ethics (20%).
